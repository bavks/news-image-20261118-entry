# news-image-20261118 — the world on Wednesday, in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 18 November's world news in one
image"** (news-image-20261118).

## What it does

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` to the output directory
(`/output` by default, or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set). It uses only
the Python standard library, needs no network, and writes byte-identical
output on every run.

## The image

A newspaper front page in colour for **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**,
typeset as "The Daily Meridian": a deep-red masthead and dateline over
one lead story (Iran's strikes on U.S. bases in the Gulf) and five more
stories in ruled columns, each with a short headline and one factual
sub-line. The illustrations are drawn in code in flat newsprint
colours, and all lettering was typeset programmatically (no generative
text), so nothing is garbled and the page stays legible shrunk to
300 px across. `render_image.py` is the renderer that produces
`entry.png`.

## The six stories, as reported on the day

1. **Iran strikes U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain** — Gulf states
   said on Wednesday they intercepted missiles and drones from Iran
   and its Houthi allies; Iran's army said it targeted U.S. drone and
   missile installations at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem base, and Bahrain
   intercepted Iranian attacks for the third time this week — hours
   after U.S. fighter jets struck a Revolutionary Guards missile
   launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz.
   *Source: Reuters, 18 Nov 2026.*
2. **Xi greets Trump in Shenzhen** — China's Xi Jinping made a
   surprise tarmac greeting for Donald Trump in Shenzhen on Wednesday
   and met Vladimir Putin, as the three leaders gathered ahead of
   Thursday's first-ever trilateral summit at APEC; Xi pledged to
   stand against trade protectionism.
   *Sources: New York Post (Reuters/AFP photographs), 18 Nov 2026;
   Zark News daily briefing, 18 Nov 2026.*
3. **Peltola flips Alaska Senate seat** — Democrat Mary Peltola beat
   Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in Alaska's U.S. Senate race, the
   final midterm contest to be called, giving Democrats a 56-44
   Senate majority, their largest since 2009; she is the first Alaska
   Native elected to the Senate.
   *Sources: Reuters, published 18 Nov 2026; Associated Press race
   call via CNN and WSJ, 18 Nov 2026.*
4. **U.S. loses measles-free status** — The United States lost its
   measles elimination status, held for 26 years, after a verification
   commission found the virus had spread continuously for more than a
   year; the country has recorded 5,412 confirmed cases and 10 deaths
   in 2026.
   *Sources: Zark News daily briefing, 18 Nov 2026; Horizon world
   news, 18 Nov 2026.*
5. **Congo Ebola cases pass 11,000** — The Ebola outbreak in the
   Democratic Republic of Congo has more than doubled in three months
   to more than 11,000 cases with 5,400 deaths, making it the
   second-largest Ebola outbreak on record; children are bearing the
   heaviest toll.
   *Sources: Zark News daily briefing, 18 Nov 2026; One Turn world
   news, 18 Nov 2026; Horizon world news, 18 Nov 2026.*
6. **Voyager 1 reaches one light-day** — NASA's Voyager 1, launched
   in 1977, became the first human-made object to reach one light-day
   from Earth — about 16.1 billion miles — at 5:16 a.m. ET on
   Wednesday, so its radio signals now take a full 24 hours to
   arrive.
   *Sources: Reuters, 18 Nov 2026; ScienceAlert, 18 Nov 2026;
   USA Today, 18 Nov 2026.*

Stories considered and dropped after date-checking: a "Spain train
crash kills dozens in Toledo" item circulating in 18 November roundups
turned out to be the 28 October 2026 Urda collision being recycled
with fresh dates, so it was excluded; the EU's provisional deal to
speed renewable-energy permits (Reuters, 18 Nov 2026) and a Russian
military transport crash in Siberia that killed 19 were verified but
edged out on consequence.
